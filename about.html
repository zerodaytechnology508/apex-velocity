About Apex Velocity

Apex Velocity is a free 3D circuit racing game that runs directly in your web browser, with nothing to download or install. You line up sixth on the grid at the 2.7 km Valle Ridge Circuit and race five AI drivers over one, three or five laps.

How to play

Wait for the five red start lights to go out, then accelerate with W or the up arrow. Brake before corners with S, steer with A and D, and use Space for the handbrake when you want the rear to slide. Hold Shift to fire nitro on the straights; it refills slowly while you drive. Press R if you get stuck, C to switch camera and P to pause.

Racing tips

Brake in a straight line before the corner, then turn in. Running onto the grass halves your top speed, and hitting the barriers costs momentum, so a clean lap is faster than a brave one. The red-and-white curbs mark the corners: aim for the inside curb at the apex.

Graphics settings

Fast suits phones and older laptops. High adds soft shadows, bloom and multisample anti-aliasing. Ultra 4K renders the race at up to 3840 pixels wide with 4096-pixel shadows, for desktop graphics cards.

Requirements

A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari with WebGL 2. Gamepads are supported. On touch screens, on-screen pedals and steering appear automatically.
